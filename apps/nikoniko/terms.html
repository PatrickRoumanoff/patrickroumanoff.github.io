The service

CloudScript.io NikoNiko Calendar is a macro for Confluence Cloud. Placed on a page, it renders a calendar grid for a list of people the page's editor configures, over a date range the configuration sets. Each person can record one value per day for themselves, chosen from a scale of either three or five options, and the grid shows those values for the configured people across the range. The macro can also show a team trend row, derived at render time from the values already displayed in the grid, which is on by default and can be turned off in the configuration.

The macro offers three visual themes and is available in English and French. A single macro instance shows up to 50 people over a window of up to 35 days. People are added through a picker that searches your Confluence site's user directory.

The app records four items per entry: the Atlassian account identifier of the person the entry belongs to, the date, the value entered, and the time the entry was written. It stores no free-text content of any kind. Display names and avatars are read from Confluence each time the macro renders and are not stored by the app. The app makes no network request to any host outside Atlassian's platform. What the app stores, where it is stored, and how long it is kept are described in the Privacy Policy.

Termination

You may stop using the app at any time by uninstalling it from your Confluence site. On uninstall, the app's stored entries are removed by Atlassian in line with the Forge hosted storage data lifecycle, described in Atlassian's own documentation at https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/forge/storage-reference/hosted-storage-data-lifecycle/. We hold no copy of that data anywhere else, because the app writes to no store other than Forge hosted storage on your own site.

Separately from uninstall, where Atlassian reports through its personal-data reporting flow that an Atlassian account has been closed, the app erases every entry belonging to that account within one reporting cycle. That cycle runs every seven days.

The app provides no export function, so entries cannot be exported in a structured format from within the app. Values are visible in the rendered calendar on the Confluence page while the app is installed and licensed.
