The service

Tech Adoption Radar for Confluence is a Confluence Cloud macro. It renders a technology-adoption radar from a blip list that you paste into the macro configuration as CSV or JSON, accepting both the Thoughtworks build-your-own-radar CSV convention and the Zalando radar.js JSON dialect. The radar is drawn client-side as a quadrant chart directly in the Confluence page, with movement markers and a numbered legend grouped by quadrant and ring. Blips may carry same-site links that open the referenced Confluence content. The macro can export the radar to PDF or Word as a structured table by quadrant and ring, and offers a client-side download of the chart as an image. The app runs with no Forge scopes and makes no network requests to any host outside Atlassian, and it stores nothing outside Confluence's own page content.
